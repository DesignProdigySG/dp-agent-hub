# Format: short video script (30–60 seconds)

For social or sales outreach. About 75–150 spoken words. Written to be said aloud by a real
person (a client expert or salesperson), not a voice-over brochure.

## Structure (with rough timings)
| Time | Beat | What's said | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Hook | Names the moment or disrupts a belief. | Text overlay of the hook |
| 3–15s | Field note | What usually happens to a <persona> in this moment, in tribe terms. | Speaker to camera |
| 15–35s | Insight | The diagnostic insight (the reveal). One proof point. | Simple visual or stat card |
| 35–50s | The gap | What they should check in their own setup. | Checklist overlay |
| 50–60s | CTA | Low-friction next step. | Name, title, CTA |

## Rules
- Short sentences; no jargon outside the tribe's own terms; never the banned phrases.
- Write captions-friendly lines (most people watch muted).
- Give two alternative hooks so the team can test them.

## By buying stage
- Status Quo Break: "the cost nobody's counting" angle.
- Solution Scramble: "three questions to ask any vendor" angle.
- Internal Sell: "how <peer> justified it to their CFO" angle.
- Final Hesitation: "what the first 30 days actually look like" angle.
