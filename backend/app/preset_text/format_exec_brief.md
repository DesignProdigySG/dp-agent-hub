# Format: one-page executive briefing

For arming the champion at the Internal Sell stage: something they can forward to the CFO, IT
and procurement without rewriting. About 400–550 words, scannable, in Markdown.

## Structure
1. **Title**: the decision in plain words (e.g. "Replacing the 2021 laptop fleet before the
   Q2 expansion").
2. **The situation (2–3 lines)**: the moment and why now, with the evidence.
3. **What it costs to wait**: 2–4 bullets in the reader's KPIs (downtime hours, support
   tickets, security exposure, cost per seat). Only use numbers from the Proof Bank or the
   brief; otherwise show the formula with placeholders, e.g. "{seats} × {hours lost} ×
   {hourly cost}".
4. **Options considered**: 2–3 rows (including doing nothing), with honest trade-offs.
5. **Recommended option**: why, in the language of each committee member (finance, IT,
   operations), one line each.
6. **Proof**: 2–3 items from the Proof Bank (stat, peer quote, certification).
7. **Risks and how they're handled**: implementation, migration, support.
8. **Next step**: the decision needed and by when.

## Rules
- No hype words. Every claim is sourced or labelled as an estimate.
- Leave space for the champion's own numbers; mark them as {placeholders}.
