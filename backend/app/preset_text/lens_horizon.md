# Lens 1: the reader's horizon

Goal: what this persona already believes, the baggage they bring, and the clichés they tune out.

## Steps
1. **Competitor clichés.** Find the client's top 3 competitors (from the brief, or search
   "<category> vendors <region>"). Read each homepage with fetch_page. List the phrases at least
   two of them share ("seamless", "next-gen", "empower", "all-in-one"...). These are the
   **banned phrases** for this client.
2. **Buyer complaints.** Search for 2–3 star reviews of the competitors and the category:
   - "<competitor> reviews G2", "<competitor> Capterra cons", "<category> TrustRadius"
   - "<category> complaints reddit", "<competitor> problems"
   Read what you can (some review sites block automated readers; use whatever loads, including
   review snippets in search results, and say which sources you could not read).
   Note recurring complaints in the buyer's own words.
3. **Beliefs.** From steps 1–2 write what the buying committee believes is true about the
   category ("all vendors overpromise on battery life", "support disappears after the sale").

## Output section
```
## Horizon
**What they already believe:** 3–6 bullets, each with a source link
**Past burns, in their words:** 3–6 short quotes or paraphrases, each with a source link
**Banned phrases (competitor clichés):** list
**First-line strategy:** acknowledge or disrupt which belief, and how
```
