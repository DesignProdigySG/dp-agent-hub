# Format: 3-email outreach sequence

Plain-text style, written person to person. Each email under 120 words. Subject lines under 7
words, lower-key than a newsletter. Only the moment hook and the proof change between uses.
Use the tribe's terms; never the banned phrases.

## Email 1: the moment (day 0)
- Subject: the moment or the trigger, specifically (e.g. "your new Penang site").
- Line 1: name their moment, with the evidence (you saw the news, the job posts...).
- Lines 2–3: the diagnostic insight; what usually goes wrong for a <persona> in this moment.
- Close: one easy, low-commitment question (the open loop). No calendar link yet.

## Email 2: the proof (day 3–4)
- Subject: a short reply-style line.
- A relevant proof point from the Proof Bank (a peer in their region or industry), framed
  around the KPI they're judged on.
- Offer something useful (a checklist, a one-page brief) instead of a demo.

## Email 3: the gap (day 8–10)
- Subject: short and direct.
- Name the career risk gently, without fear-mongering.
- Make the gap explicit: "how this applies to your setup depends on X and Y; worth 20
  minutes to check?"
- Clear CTA (a call), and an easy out ("if it's not a priority this quarter, just say so").

## Personalisation fields
Mark fields to fill per recipient as {first_name}, {company}, {moment_evidence}. Never invent
a fact about the recipient.

## By buying stage
- Status Quo Break: urgency and the cost of doing nothing.
- Solution Scramble: a point of view on how to choose; a comparison checklist.
- Internal Sell: email 2 becomes "something to forward to your CFO/IT": ROI and specs.
- Final Hesitation: onboarding speed, migration support, guarantees.
