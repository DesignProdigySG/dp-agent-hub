# Lens 4: the strategic gap

Goal: what the message reveals and what it deliberately holds back, so the next step (a reply
or a booked call) feels like the natural way to close the loop.

## Steps
1. **Read the client's own material.** The client deck or brief attached to the task
   (read_attachment / search_attachments), the client's website, and anything already saved
   for the client (find_client_docs, e.g. the Proof Bank).
2. **Find the diagnostic insight.** One sharp, specific, non-obvious problem the client can
   name better than anyone, ideally tied to the moment from lens 2 and the KPIs from lens 3.
   Prefer something the reader can check against their own company.
3. **Decide what to hold back.** The "how it applies to your setup" part: the specific
   diagnosis, the numbers for their fleet, the migration plan. That is what the call is for.
4. **Check:** does the reveal stand on its own without the gap? Does the gap make the reader
   want the next step rather than feel withheld from?

## Output section
```
## Strategic gap
**Reveal (the insight):** 1–2 sentences, with the proof it rests on
**Hold back:** what is left for the conversation
**Next step it earns:** the call to action, in the reader's terms
```
