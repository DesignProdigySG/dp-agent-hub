# B2B Moments Library

A moment is a change that makes a buyer more likely to act now. Each family lists example
triggers, how to detect them with web search, and the buying stage it usually opens.

## 1. Macro and industry moments
Affect every company in a sector or market at once.
- **New regulation or deadline.** Examples in APAC: Singapore PDPA amendments and MAS Technology
  Risk Management guidelines, Malaysia's PDPA amendments, Indonesia's Personal Data Protection
  Law, Thailand's PDPA, Vietnam's Decree 13, e-invoicing mandates (e.g. Malaysia's phased
  e-Invoice rollout).
  Detect: search "<country> <topic> regulation <year>", "<regulator> guidelines", freshness pm.
- **Budget planning cycles.** Many SEA companies plan budgets in Q3–Q4 for a January financial
  year; Japanese-owned firms often plan for an April year; government agencies follow their
  own fiscal year (Singapore: April). Grants such as Singapore's productivity and digitalisation
  schemes have their own windows.
  Detect: the company's financial year in its annual report; "<scheme name> grant <year>".
- **Major industry events and conferences** (e.g. regional tech weeks, trade shows).
  Detect: "<industry> conference <city> <year>"; speaker lists name active companies.
- **Market shocks:** supply-chain disruption, a big competitor's exit, currency moves.
Usual stage: Status Quo Break.

## 2. Account-level moments
Specific to one company.
- **Leadership change:** a new CIO, CFO, CTO, VP or country head (first 90 days: wants quick,
  safe wins).
- **Funding round, IPO or listing.**
- **Merger, acquisition or new regional entity** (systems must be integrated or standardised).
- **Expansion:** a new office, factory, warehouse or market entry.
- **Hiring spike:** many open roles in one function (e.g. 15 sales roles means devices,
  onboarding and tools are needed).
- **Security incident, outage or failed audit** (handle with care; never gloat).
- **Award, big contract win or public tender** won or issued.
Detect: "<company> appoints", "<company> new CEO OR CIO OR CFO", "<company> acquires",
"<company> funding", "<company> expands <country>", "<company> careers <function>", news with
freshness pw or pm. Verify on the company's own newsroom or a reputable outlet.
Usual stage: Status Quo Break, sometimes Solution Scramble.

## 3. Intent and behavioural moments
The buyer is actively looking.
- High-intent searches, pricing-page visits, competitor comparison searches, review-site
  activity, webinar attendance, RFP or tender notices.
Detect: public tenders (e.g. GeBIZ in Singapore), RFP announcements, posts asking for vendor
recommendations on forums. First-party signals (site visits, email clicks) come from the
client's own tools, not the web.
Usual stage: Solution Scramble.

## 4. Lifecycle moments
For existing customers of the client.
- Onboarding milestones, usage drop-offs, 90 days before renewal, a new product release they
  should adopt, the end of a hardware refresh cycle (often 3–4 years for laptops).
Detect: from the client's CRM or Marketo, not the web.
Usual stage: Internal Sell or Final Hesitation.

## Moment card (the standard shape)

| Field | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Account | Company name and website |
| Moment | Family and trigger, in one line |
| Evidence | The link you read and its date |
| Who it affects | The persona and why |
| Likely stage | Status Quo Break, Solution Scramble, Internal Sell or Final Hesitation |
| Hook | One sentence that speaks to that reader in that moment |
| Format | The content kit item that fits best |
| Strength | Strong, medium or weak, with the reason |
