# Lens 2: the moment lens

Goal: the triggers that make this persona start caring, and how they read when it happens.

## Steps
1. **Case-study "before" paragraphs.** Search for the client's and competitors' case studies:
   "<client> case study", "<competitor> customer story <industry>". Read 4–6 with fetch_page.
   Ignore the results; copy the trigger from the opening ("after a regional expansion", "when
   their audit flagged...", "following a merger").
2. **Forums.** Search Reddit and niche forums for the persona's pain in their own words:
   "site:reddit.com <role> <pain>", r/sysadmin, r/ITManagers, r/CFO, r/salesoperations, and
   regional forums or communities where they exist. Note what made them post.
3. **Match to the Moments Library** (search_knowledge "B2B Moments Library") and the buying
   stage table in the Reader-First Framework.
4. For each trigger decide the **reading posture**: panicked skim (needs fast reassurance and
   a checklist) or calm evaluation (needs ROI maths and architecture proof).

## Output section
```
## Moments
| Trigger | Moment family | Buying stage | Reading posture | What they need first | Source |
(4–8 rows)
**The moment to lead with:** one, and why
```
