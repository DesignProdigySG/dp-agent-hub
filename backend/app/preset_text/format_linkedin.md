# Format: LinkedIn post or carousel

Only the **hook** (from the moment) and the **proof** (from the Proof Bank) change between
uses. Write as a peer sharing field notes. Use the tribe's insider terms; never the banned
phrases.

## Single post (120–220 words)
1. **Hook line** (under 15 words): names the moment or disrupts a belief from the horizon. No
   question openers like "Are you struggling with...?".
2. **Field note** (2–3 short lines): what typically happens in this moment, in tribe language.
3. **Diagnostic insight** (the reveal from the strategic gap).
4. **One proof point**, sourced from the Proof Bank.
5. **Open loop + soft CTA**: leave the "how it applies to you" open; invite a reply or DM.
No more than 3 hashtags; none is fine.

## Carousel (6–8 slides, max 25 words per slide)
1. Cover: the hook.
2. The moment: "When <trigger> happens...".
3. What most teams do (the status quo / baggage).
4. Why it fails, in their KPI terms.
5. The insight.
6. Proof (a stat or quote, with the customer type).
7. What to check in your own setup (one diagnostic question; the gap).
8. CTA.
Give each slide's text, plus a one-line visual note for the designer.

## By buying stage
- Status Quo Break: lead with the hidden cost of doing nothing.
- Solution Scramble: call out where "traditional" options fail; take a clear point of view.
- Internal Sell: a carousel the champion can forward: ROI maths, security specs, peer proof.
- Final Hesitation: rarely LinkedIn; if used, focus on smooth rollout stories.
