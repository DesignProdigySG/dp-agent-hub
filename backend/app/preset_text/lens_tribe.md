# Lens 3: the interpretive community

Goal: the tribe's language, the KPIs they are judged on, and their hidden career risk.

## Steps
1. **Job descriptions.** Search for 3–4 current job posts for the persona in the client's target
   regions: "<role> job <city>", "<role> responsibilities <industry> Singapore", company
   careers pages and job boards that allow reading (skip LinkedIn). Read them with fetch_page.
2. From the responsibilities and requirements, pull:
   - **KPIs** they are measured on (uptime, cost per seat, device refresh cycle, ticket
     resolution time...)
   - **Insider terms**: 3–5 specific terms, standards, tools or workflows (e.g. "MDM", "TCO
     per seat", "ISO 27001", "imaging", "SOC 2 Type II"). Never generic phrases like "optimise
     operations".
3. **Career risk.** What would make this person look bad in front of their boss? Derive it from
   the KPIs (a failed rollout, a security incident on a lost laptop, overspending the refresh
   budget). Label it as an inference.
4. **Voice.** A short note on how a peer in this tribe talks: field notes, not a brochure.

## Output section
```
## Tribe
**KPIs they're judged on:** bullets, each with the job post link
**Insider terms to use:** 3–5, with a one-line meaning each
**Hidden career risk (inference):** 1–3 bullets
**How a peer would say it:** 2–3 example sentences
```
